Entertainment

Spartan Fall Festival

October 2 at 5:00 p.m.

University Lawn

The Spartan Fall Festival brings students together for an evening of music, food, games, and campus activities. Students can meet organizations, enjoy live entertainment, and celebrate the fall season with friends.

What to Expect

The festival offers activities for students with a variety of interests. Attendees can listen to music, visit organization tables, play games, and purchase food from campus vendors.

Festival Activities

  • Live music performed by student groups
  • Food and drinks from campus vendors
  • Outdoor games and friendly competitions
  • Information tables from student organizations
  • A fall-themed photo area

Event Schedule

  • 5:00 p.m. — Festival opens
  • 5:30 p.m. — Student organization activities begin
  • 6:00 p.m. — Live music begins
  • 7:30 p.m. — Campus games and prizes
  • 9:00 p.m. — Festival ends

Accessibility and Participation

The University Lawn includes accessible walking paths and seating areas. Students who need accommodations can contact the event organizer before attending. Participation in festival activities is optional, and students may attend for any portion of the event.
